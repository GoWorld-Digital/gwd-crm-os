# GWD Adoption & Portfolio Status — CRM Codebase

**Status:** PRODUCT CODEBASE / VENTURE CANDIDATE INPUT  
**Baseline:** 20 November 2026

This repository contains a CRM and WhatsApp-oriented sales product codebase derived from the DeskcommCRM project. Its code maturity, public availability, existing branding or upstream integrations do not automatically make it an approved GWD Venture or the GWD-wide operating system.

## Current interpretation

- Preserve the codebase as a separate product asset for technical and commercial evaluation.
- The business opportunity, ownership model, target customer, monetization and operating model must be assessed through the GWD Venture Portfolio.
- GWD may eventually operate the product, license it, provide implementation/managed services, or decide not to pursue it.
- Do not assume that GWD owns the upstream product, its trademarks, partner relationships, customer base or commercial rights.
- Review upstream license, contributor history, third-party services, security posture, privacy obligations and public repository contents before deployment or commercial distribution.
- Do not connect customer data, WhatsApp accounts, production credentials or payment services without a separately approved mandate and technical controls.
- Do not represent the codebase as a deployed GWD CRM or as an approved Venture until the evidence and Council decision exist.
